Griffininteractive Design Discovery

This is a private, single-user design-discovery utility operated by Griffininteractive.

The tool reads Pins and boards from the authenticated account owner's own Pinterest account and imports visual references into that person's personal Figma workspace as individual design references.

It does not provide a public Pinterest service, publish Pins, modify Pinterest content, or provide access to other users' Pinterest data.
