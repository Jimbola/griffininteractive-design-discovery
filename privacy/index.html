Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 September 2026

Griffininteractive Design Discovery is a private, single-user design-discovery utility. It is used only by the account owner to bring visual references from that person's own Pinterest boards into a personal Figma workspace.

Information accessed

The tool may access Pinterest account information required to identify the authenticated user and read that user's own Pins and boards, including Pin identifiers, board identifiers, Pin images and source URLs.

How the information is used

The information is used only to identify Pins from the account owner's boards and import their visual references into that owner's personal Figma workspace. The tool does not use Pinterest data for advertising, profiling, analytics, resale or unrelated purposes.

Sharing

Pinterest data accessed by the tool is not sold, rented or shared with third parties for their own use.

Storage

The tool is designed for personal use and does not operate a separate user database. Imported design references may remain in the account owner's Figma file. Authentication tokens are not intentionally written into the Figma document.

Other users

The tool is not offered as a service to other users and is not intended to access other people's private Pinterest data.

Changes

This policy may be updated if the tool's behaviour changes. The current version will be published on this page.

Contact

For questions about this privacy policy, contact Griffininteractive through the repository associated with this site.
